You promptly signed up for the **League of Heroes** superhero team. They gave you the hero name **Free-Lancer**. You asked about getting a lance; they said the emphasis was on the “free” part.

The dreaded **Dr. Breach** has wrecked the League's roster data. The League needs an audit report before it can send heroes on missions.

```txt
League of Heroes Roster Audit

Heroes on roster: 4
Average power level: 15.75
First responder: Scuba Driver
Newest recruit: Neon Volt
Audit status: Ready for review
```

By the end of this project, you will rebuild the League's data until it can produce a report like that using real information from the roster.

<Callout type="note" title="Your mission">
  You have already learned every JavaScript tool you will need for this project.

  This is not about remembering every method perfectly without help. It is about looking at a problem, deciding what kind of data you need, and choosing the right tool to work with it.
</Callout>

## Step 1: Primitives

The League has sent you this information about one of their members:

> His name is The Scuba Driver. He is currently active. His power level is 5. His age is 20. His power is "Underwater Transportation" (whatever that is), and he doesn't have a secret identity. He just *is* The Scuba Driver. He's based out of "New Atlantis."

Create variables for:

- `heroName`
- `isActive`
- `powerLevel`
- `age`
- `power`
- `secretIdentity`
- `city`

Think carefully about the value each variable needs.

## Step 2: Build the Profile Message

Create a variable called `profileMessage`. Assign it a template literal that uses the variables you just created:

```txt
The Scuba Driver has the power of Underwater Transportation with a power level of 5.
```

Then log `profileMessage`.
