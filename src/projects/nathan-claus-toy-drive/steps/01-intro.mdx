# Nathan Claus' Toy Drive (No Relation)

Nathan Claus (no relation) runs a tiny nonprofit called **Operation: Nice List**. The donors are generous, the spreadsheets are relentless, and the deadline is tonight. Your mission is to finish a console-first toy assignment system that proves every nice kid received something delightful.

## What You Are Building

- Filter a mixed list of people down to the kids who actually qualify.
- Assign toys using a mix of requested gifts and random picks.
- Record every assignment in a reusable data structure.
- Print a readable report so Nathan can breathe again.

## Starter Sandbox

The sandbox ships with:

- `people`, `toys`, and `kidToys` arrays.
- Helper stubs such as `isEligibleForGift`, `createAssignmentRecord`, and `assignGift`.
- Console checkpoints you can uncomment as you progress.

Keep everything in `main.js` for now. Modularization comes later in the curriculum.
