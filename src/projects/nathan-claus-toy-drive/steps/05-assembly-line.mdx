# Step 4 — The Assembly Line

Scale the operation from one kid to the entire list. Use `runToyDrive()` to loop every person, qualify them, and assign gifts when appropriate.

## Requirements

- Iterate with `for...of` over `people`.
- Use `isEligibleForGift` to check that the person qualifies for a gift
- Call `assignGift` for every _**qualifying**_ kid.
- Use temporary `console.log()`s to make sure they were added successfully.

## Checkpoint

After running `runToyDrive()`:

- `kidToys.length` should equal the number of qualifying kids.
- Spot-check a couple of records to ensure requested toys were honored when possible.

When the assembly line runs smoothly, you are ready to print the receipt.
