# Stretch Goal — Elf Level

Give the receipt printer an upgrade by swapping your nested loops for modern array helpers. Research the [`Array.prototype.find()`](https://www.w3schools.com/jsref/jsref_find.asp) method and use it to locate the matching kid and toy when printing assignments.

## What to Explore

- Read the W3Schools reference on [`find()`](https://www.w3schools.com/jsref/jsref_find.asp) to understand how it returns the first matching element.
- Replace the loops that look up a kid and a toy in `printAssignments()` with `.find()` calls on the `people` and `toys` arrays.
- Keep your fallback behavior intact: if a lookup fails, warn Nathan instead of crashing the report.
